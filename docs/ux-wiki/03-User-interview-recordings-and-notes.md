## Content

### Research plan (goal, questions, participants, schedule)

- **Method:** an online survey (Google Form) with mostly open questions about a real recent game, followed by 15–20 minute chats with people who agree to one. _Trade-off:_ a form can't ask follow-up "why" questions, so the chats fill that gap.
- **Goal:** answer the doubts and check the suppositions in [#2](02-Business-goals-and-constraints):
  - Do players look back at their games, and how?
  - What do they want to find out, and where do they get stuck?
  - Do they make, show or share stats and charts with others?
  - What data do they reach for first?
- **Participants:** AoE4 players, starting with iman244's friends. Aim for 5 or more answers per round, and 3–5 follow-up chats.
- **Form:** [How do you look back at your AoE4 games?](https://docs.google.com/forms/d/e/1FAIpQLSdba1arnmcmGjZ2pfCYjE4TOoTlfkYZ1HDLS7r0bEvYoR82rA/viewform). Built by iman244 and checked against this guide on 2026-10-22. Short link: https://forms.gle/QgNATVdnwyZCesap9
- **Where it was shared:** Twitch chat on Beasty's stream (posted by a moderator for iman244, 2026-10-22); r/aoe4 planned (Rule 3 allows one self-promotion post every 3 days); Siege Engineer Discord suggested.
- **Schedule:** the form is ready to send. Chats follow as people sign up.

### Interview guide

**Form intro text:**
> We're designing a free tool for looking at Age of Empires IV games. This takes about 5 minutes. Your answers are only used to design the tool, and we won't share your contact details.

**Section 1: About you**

1. How often do you play AoE4? _(one choice: every day / a few times a week / about once a week / less often)_
2. Which modes do you play most? _(checkboxes: ranked 1v1 / ranked team games / quick match / custom games / other)_
3. What's your rank in your main mode? _(dropdown: Unranked, Bronze, Silver, Gold, Platinum, Diamond, Conqueror)_

**Section 2: Looking back at your games**

4. After a game, how often do you look back at what happened? _(one choice: after every game / after most games / sometimes / rarely / never. "Never" skips to Section 3. This is a multiple choice question because a scale can't skip sections in Google Forms.)_
5. Think of the last game you looked back at. What did you want to find out? _(paragraph)_
6. Where did you look? _(checkboxes: in-game stats screen / aoe4world / the replay / a video or stream / talking it over with friends / other)_
7. What happened next? Did you talk about it with anyone, and how? _(paragraph)_
8. What was hard or missing when you tried to find it out? _(paragraph)_

**Section 3: Stats, charts and sharing**

9. Have you ever made your own chart, note or spreadsheet about your games? If so, what did it show? _(paragraph)_
10. Have you ever shown someone a stat or chart to make a point about a game? Tell us what happened. _(paragraph)_
11. Have you watched or read someone else's breakdown of a game (a pro, caster or friend)? What made it useful or not? _(paragraph)_
12. If you could see any number or chart about your last game, what would it be? _(paragraph)_

**Section 4: Follow-up**

13. Would you be up for a 15–20 minute chat about this? If so, leave your Discord name or email. _(short answer, optional)_

**Follow-up chat guide:** start from the person's answers to questions 5, 8 and 10, and keep asking "Tell me more about that" and "Why?"
